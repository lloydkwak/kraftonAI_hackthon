usvnav course editor

+x right, +y up. Metres. Scroll to zoom, drag empty water to pan.

Add

Click a button, then click the map. Click a thing to select it; the Delete button below (or the Backspace / Delete key) removes it; [ / ] rotate it. A lane is where the moving vessels run: it is added straight across the reach at the clicked height; drag its points, insert points to bend it, set its schedule in the selection panel.

Checks
